Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Villa Terminal A Catering, operated by Villa Restaurant Group at Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you visit our catering website or contact our team.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is designed to provide catering menus, pricing, promotions, and contact information.

We do not process payments, require customer accounts, or collect order information directly through website forms.

However, when you contact us through WhatsApp or email, you may voluntarily provide personal information, including your name, phone number, email address, catering requests, and delivery details.

We use this information only as needed to respond to inquiries, coordinate catering orders, and provide customer service.

Our website hosting provider may also automatically collect basic technical information, such as IP addresses, browser types, device information, and website activity, for operational and security purposes.

2. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website does not currently include advertising trackers or analytics scripts in its supplied HTML.

However, our hosting provider may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, performance, and security.

If analytics tools are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to explain their use.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information voluntarily provided through WhatsApp or email may be used to:

  • Respond to catering inquiries.
  • Confirm menu selections, pricing, and availability.
  • Coordinate catering orders and delivery arrangements.
  • Provide customer support and communicate important order updates.

We do not sell customer personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website provides links to external communication platforms, including WhatsApp, as well as email applications.

When you choose to communicate through these services, your information is handled according to the privacy policies and practices of the respective service providers.

We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

5. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information shared with our catering team.

However, communications over the internet and third-party messaging platforms cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please avoid sharing sensitive financial information, passwords, or payment card details through WhatsApp or email unless specifically directed to an approved secure payment method.

6. Information Retention

Information shared through email or WhatsApp may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage catering orders, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for general catering information and business-related inquiries.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through our website.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in website functionality, business operations, or applicable privacy requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this website with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact our catering team.
